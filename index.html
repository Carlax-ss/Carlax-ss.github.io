Mechanic Master

A cheeky 1970s garage puzzle game

It's 1976, the radio's playing funk and there's a queue outside your little garage. Every car that rolls onto your lift is a puzzle:

  • Sort brake pads, filters and spark plugs onto the right racks
  • Pour oil and coolant between jugs without mixing them
  • Spin coloured nuts onto the matching bolts and fit every wheel
  • Drain the sump, fix windscreen chips and wire the fuse box

200 jobs, a cast of cheeky regulars with their own stories, garage upgrades to earn and a 1976 pin-up calendar to collect.
