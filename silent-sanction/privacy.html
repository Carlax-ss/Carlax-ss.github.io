Silent Sanction: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Silent Sanction ("the game") on Android, iPhone, iPad or Amazon Fire. The game is for players aged 16 and over.

Information the game itself stores

Your progress, unlocks and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and no chat, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or messages.

Adverts

The game shows a short advert between some contracts and offers optional adverts you can choose to watch for extra intel. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the adverts between contracts. Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), your device type and how you interact with adverts, to show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form when you first play, and you can choose to allow or refuse personalised adverts. You can change your choice at any time from the game's privacy settings. On iPhone and iPad the game asks your permission before your device's advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's settings.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as removing adverts or unlocking all districts) are handled by Google Play (Android), the App Store (iPhone and iPad) or the Amazon Appstore (Fire). We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not made for children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email mechanicmasterlive@proton.me.
