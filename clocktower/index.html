Clocktower Stables

A horse and pony care game for ages 6–14, with real breeds and real-world horse care.

  • Muck out, groom, feed and water your horses, and meet the farrier
  • Dress your rider and choose saddle pads and kit in the tack room
  • Ride a dressage test, jump a show jumping course and race on the flat or over hedges
  • Win rosettes and trophies, and save up for new horses

Everything is unlocked just by playing. No chat, no accounts, no personal information collected.
