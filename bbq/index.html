Smoke Ring: BBQ Showdown

16+. A barbecue cooking-competition game.

Fire up the pit and cook to order: Texas brisket, Carolina whole hog, Korean BBQ, asado and more. Pick your cooker, wood, rub and sauce, manage the fire and the vents, then pull the meat at just the right moment. Secret judges sit at random tables, and every plate is scored on appearance, taste and tenderness.

  • Low-and-slow smoking and hot-and-fast grilling, with real cookers that behave differently
  • Competitions around the world, with a live crowd and rival pitmasters
  • Spend your prize money on new cookers, woods, rubs, sauces and tools
  • Mild bad language, set to Full, Bleeped or Clean
