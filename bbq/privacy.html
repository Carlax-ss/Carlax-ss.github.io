Smoke Ring: BBQ Showdown: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Smoke Ring: BBQ Showdown ("the game") on Android, iPhone, iPad or Amazon Fire. The game is for players aged 16 and over.

Information the game itself stores

Your progress, prize money, unlocks, settings and your answer to the age check are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and no chat with real people, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or messages.

Adverts

On Android, iPhone and iPad, the game shows adverts between some competitions and offers optional adverts you can choose to watch for a reward. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the adverts between competitions. The Amazon Fire version uses no Google services and shows no adverts.

Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), your device type and how you interact with adverts, to show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and if you don't give it you are shown only non-personalised adverts. On iPhone and iPad you are asked before any tracking. You can change your choice at any time from the settings screen, and reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, the App Store or Amazon. We never see or store your payment details.

Age

The game is for people aged 16 and over, and it asks your age when you first open it. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect any information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email mechanicmasterlive@proton.me.
