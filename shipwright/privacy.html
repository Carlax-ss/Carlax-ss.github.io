Shipwright: Build & Battle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Shipwright: Build & Battle ("the game") on Android or iPhone.

Information the game itself stores

Your designs, progress, unlocks and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and no chat, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or messages. Blueprint share codes only describe a vehicle design; they contain no personal information.

Adverts (optional)

The game can show an advert only when you choose to watch one, for example for a bonus reward. Nothing in the game requires watching adverts.

Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), your device type and how you interact with adverts, to show adverts and measure them. Players who tell us they are under 13, and players in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland who don't give consent, are shown only non-personalised adverts, and AdMob is set to treat those requests as child-directed. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's settings, and on iPhone the game asks before any tracking.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

If the game offers optional purchases, they are handled by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). We never see or store your payment details. Nothing that affects winning is ever sold.

Children

The game is made for players aged 9 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email mechanicmasterlive@proton.me.
