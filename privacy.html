Mechanic Master: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Mechanic Master ("the game") on Android or iPhone.

Information the game itself stores

Your progress, scores, upgrades and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or messages.

Advertising

The game shows adverts using Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), your device type, and how you interact with adverts, to show adverts and measure them.

In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland you're asked for your consent before personalised adverts are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings on the game's Home screen. On iPhone you're also asked whether to allow tracking, and you can change this in your iPhone's Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's settings.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone). We never see or store your payment details. The store tells the game which items you own so they can be restored on a new device.

Children

The game is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play or App Store page.
