Glam Showdown

Styling-competition games for grown-up fashion fans (16+). Get the brief, style your model, then face the judges and the live chat.

The series

  • Glam Showdown: Nails: jewel-like nail art, colours, gems and finishes
  • Glam Showdown: Outfit: head-to-toe looks, shoes included, then the catwalk
  • Glam Showdown: Hair: cuts, colours and styling for the big salon reveal

Every round is a new brief. Three AI judges score how close you got, the stream chat reacts live, and placing well earns coins to unlock new tools, colours and items.
