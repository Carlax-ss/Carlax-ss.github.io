Glam Showdown: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy covers Glam Showdown: Nails, Glam Showdown: Outfit and Glam Showdown: Hair ("the games") on Android, iPhone, iPad and Amazon Fire. It explains what information is collected when you play.

Information the games themselves store

Your looks, progress, coins, unlocks and settings are saved only on your device. The games have no accounts, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or messages. The judges and the "stream chat" are computer-generated; there is no chat with real people.

Adverts

On Android, iPhone and iPad, the games show an advert between some rounds and offer optional adverts you can choose to watch for a reward. The optional "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the adverts between rounds. The Amazon Fire versions use no Google services.

Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), your device type and how you interact with adverts, to show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and if you don't give it you are shown only non-personalised adverts. On iPhone and iPad you are asked before any tracking. You can change your choice at any time from the settings screen, and reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads or a tip for the developer) are handled entirely by Google Play, the App Store or Amazon. We never see or store your payment details.

Age

The games are made for players aged 16 and over and are not directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email mechanicmasterlive@proton.me.
