Shipwright: Shore Defence: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Shipwright: Shore Defence ("the game") on Android, iPhone, iPad or Amazon Fire.

Information the game itself stores

Your progress, unlocks, upgrades and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and no chat, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or messages.

Adverts (optional)

The game shows adverts in two ways: an advert you choose to watch for a bonus, such as a supply drop or a second chance, and a short full-screen advert between battles (never during a battle). Buying "Remove Ads" removes the between-battle adverts and gives you those bonuses without watching. The Amazon Fire version shows no adverts.

Adverts are provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), your device type and how you interact with adverts, to show adverts, measure them and prevent fraud. Adverts are limited to content suitable for teens.

Adverts may be personalised (chosen using this information) only where that is allowed. In the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form when you first play, and you can choose to allow or refuse personalised adverts. You can change your choice at any time with the privacy settings button in the game. If you refuse, you still see adverts, but they are not personalised. On iPhone and iPad the game asks your permission (Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt) before your device's advertising identifier can be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", it isn't used, and you can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device's settings.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

If the game offers optional purchases, they are handled by Google Play (Android), the App Store (iPhone and iPad) or the Amazon Appstore (Fire). We never see or store your payment details. Nothing that affects winning is ever sold.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: email mechanicmasterlive@proton.me.
