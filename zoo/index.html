Zoo Architect: Build & Care

Design your own zoo, look after the animals, then walk round it in 3D. For ages 6–14.

  • Build enclosures, paths, cafés and play areas wherever you like
  • Feed, clean and care for zoo animals, each with the habitat they need
  • Welcome visitors and grow from a small animal park into a famous zoo
  • Take a 3D walk round everything you've built

Everything is unlocked just by playing. No chat, no accounts, no personal information collected.
