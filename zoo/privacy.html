Zoo Architect: Build & Care: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Zoo Architect: Build & Care ("the game") is a zoo-building and animal care game made for children. This policy explains, for parents and carers, what information is and isn't collected when the game is played on Android, iPhone, iPad or Amazon Fire.

The short version

  • We don't ask for, collect or store any personal information about your child.
  • There are no accounts, no chat, no sharing and no links out of the game without a grown-up check.
  • Game progress is saved only on the device.
  • On iPhone, iPad and Amazon Fire there are no adverts at all. On Android, optional adverts appear only after a grown-up check, and they are child-safe and non-personalised.

Information the game itself stores

Your child's zoo, animals, coins and settings are saved only on the device. Deleting the game deletes this information.

Adverts (Android only)

On Android, a grown-up can choose to watch a short advert for a bonus reward. These adverts are shown by Google AdMob, which is certified for use in apps for children. Every advert request is marked as directed at children, adverts are limited to the "G" (general audiences) rating, and personalised advertising, remarketing and the device's advertising identifier are not used.

To show and measure an advert and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address (used to estimate its general location), device type, app interactions with the advert, and diagnostic information. This is not used to build a profile of your child.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In-app purchases

Any optional purchases are only offered behind a grown-up check, and everything in the game can also be earned just by playing. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, the App Store or Amazon. We never see or store payment details.

Children's privacy

The game is designed to follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, the EU GDPR, Google Play's Families Policy and Apple's Kids Category rules. Because we don't collect personal information, there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete. If you believe information has been collected about your child, contact us and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
