The Big Family Quiz Show: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play The Big Family Quiz Show ("the game") on Android, Android TV, Google TV, Amazon Fire tablets, Fire TV, iPhone or iPad. The game is made for families, including children.

We don't collect any personal information

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no logins, no chat, no online play and no contact with any server we run. Player names you type in, your scores, prizes, chosen categories and settings are saved only on your device, inside the game's own storage, and are never uploaded. Deleting the game removes all of this.

No adverts, no analytics, no tracking

The game shows no adverts and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools. It does not use your device's advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Optional question packs

The game is a paid app. It also offers optional question packs as one-time purchases. Every purchase sits behind a grown-ups' check. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play, the App Store or the Amazon Appstore under their own privacy policies; we never see or store your payment details.

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. The game has no links out of the app except store pages behind the grown-ups' check.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
